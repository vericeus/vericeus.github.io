The Vericeus Manifesto

Filipino health data, for Filipinos.

Why this matters

Every day, Filipino patients are diagnosed, treated, and followed up using knowledge built somewhere else. Medical guidelines, drug trials, and now artificial intelligence are shaped mostly by data from other countries. Our patients carry their own disease patterns, speak their own languages, and live with their own realities of cost and access.

Meanwhile, the health information that could change this is scattered. It sits in paper charts, in clinics that do not share records, and in systems that cannot talk to each other. When data is fragmented, care is fragmented. Tests are repeated. Warning signs are missed. Treatments are chosen without the full picture, and families pay for it in money, time, and health.

We believe this can change. Consolidated, trustworthy health data about Filipinos is the foundation for better care in the Philippines. Artificial intelligence, used well, can turn that foundation into earlier diagnoses, smarter treatment, and lower costs for every Filipino.

What we believe

  1. Filipino patients deserve care built on Filipino evidence.

    Treatment works best when it reflects the people receiving it. Our diseases, our genetics, our languages, and our health system belong in the data that guides our care.

  2. Consolidated data is better care.

    When a patient's history follows them, doctors decide faster and with more confidence. Fewer tests are repeated, fewer conditions are missed, and treatment can be matched to what actually works for Filipinos.

  3. Better data lowers costs.

    Waste in healthcare often starts with missing information. Connected, reliable records reduce duplicate procedures, prevent avoidable complications, and help scarce resources reach the patients who need them most.

  4. AI is a powerful tool, not a replacement for care.

    Artificial intelligence can read scans, flag risks, and support overstretched health workers. Its value is measured by one thing: whether Filipino patients are better off. It must be tested on our people, held to clinical standards, and guided by clinical judgment.

  5. AI must serve every Filipino.

    The benefits of AI cannot stop at private hospitals and major cities. A rural health unit, a barangay health worker, and a patient in a remote island deserve the same quality of insight.

  6. Health data belongs to the people it describes.

    Consent, privacy, and security are not obstacles to progress. They are the conditions for it. Filipino health data should be governed in the Philippines and used for the benefit of Filipinos.

  7. Filipinos should set the priorities.

    Filipino clinicians, researchers, and communities should decide which problems AI solves first, how success is measured, and who benefits.

What we commit to

  • We will test AI against Filipino reality.

    Every system we evaluate is judged on safety, completeness, and fit with the language, diseases, and care settings of Filipino patients.

  • We will build toward consolidated, consented local data.

    We will help hospitals, researchers, and health-tech teams connect and use health data responsibly, with clear agreements, privacy safeguards, and respect for every patient.

  • We will measure success by outcomes.

    Not by how advanced a tool looks, but by whether patients are diagnosed earlier, treated more effectively, and spend less to stay well.

  • We will keep clinical judgment at the center.

    Every recommendation we make is grounded in medical practice and the realities of Philippine healthcare.

A safe and healthy future

The Philippines does not need to wait for the rest of the world to decide what good care looks like for Filipinos. We have the clinicians, the knowledge, and now the tools to build it ourselves.

When Filipino health data is consolidated, trusted, and protected, and when AI is used with care and for the good of all, every Filipino can receive treatment that is more accurate, more affordable, and more effective.

That is the future we are working toward. A safe and healthy future, built on truth in Filipino health data.
