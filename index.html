Our mission

Filipino health data, for Filipinos.

Most medical AI is trained and tested on patients from other countries. Filipino patients have their own disease patterns, languages, and realities of care, and much of their health information still sits in paper records and systems that do not connect.

Vericeus works toward consolidated, consented local health data that Philippine clinicians, researchers, and builders can trust. Data that reflects Filipino patients, governed in the Philippines, and used to improve their care.

Healthcare decisions need a clinician in the room.

Health content can sound confident and still be wrong. Medical AI can answer fluently and still miss how illness looks in a Filipino patient. Health data projects can stall on consent, privacy, and who owns what. Vericeus brings clinical judgment into each of these early, before the error is public.

What we do

Three practice areas, one clinical standard.

  • Clinical content and review

    Clinically reviewed healthcare writing, search-informed content for SEO, AEO and GEO, health-tech copy, and clinical content audits for agencies, med comms teams, and health brands.

  • Clinical AI evaluation

    Expert review of AI-generated medical answers. We help teams build rubrics, define error categories, and test for Filipino context such as Tagalog and Taglish questions, local disease burden, and Philippine clinical guidelines.

  • Health data and AI strategy

    Feasibility and planning for teams that want to use Philippine health data responsibly, including governance under the Data Privacy Act, consent, partner mapping, and pilot scoping. We work alongside privacy counsel and do not give legal advice.

What does Vericeus do?

Vericeus is a healthcare consultancy. We advise on clinical content, medical AI evaluation, and Filipino health data strategy.

Who is Vericeus for?

Healthcare agencies, med comms teams, health-tech and AI teams, hospitals, researchers, and health brands that need clinical judgment alongside clear delivery.

What is clinical AI evaluation?

A clinician reviews what an AI system says about health and scores it against agreed criteria: accuracy, safety, completeness, and fit with Philippine clinical practice. You get findings, a rubric, and recommended fixes.

Why does Vericeus focus on the Philippines?

Because Filipino patients are underrepresented in the data most medical AI learns from. Local disease patterns, languages, and care settings need local clinical judgment and, over time, consolidated local health data.

Does Vericeus work with patient data?

No, not as a starting point. We advise on whether and how health data can be used, including consent, privacy, and partners. Any data work happens only under signed agreements, ethics review where required, and privacy counsel.

Does Vericeus give medical or legal advice?

No. We do not provide patient care, diagnosis, or legal advice. We provide clinical review and consulting for organizations.

How does Vericeus ensure medical accuracy?

Every piece of work goes through a clinical review that checks clarity, structure, clinical sense, and medical accuracy, and flags claims that need a closer look.
